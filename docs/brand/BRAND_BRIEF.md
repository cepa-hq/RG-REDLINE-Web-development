# Roun Groun REDLINE — brand brief

Recorded 28 November 2026 (India time). This brief separates owner decisions from design proposals.

## Owner-confirmed direction

**Brand:** Roun Groun REDLINE, also referred to as RG REDLINE.
**Domain:** rg-redline.com.
**Relationship:** an extreme-performance brand direction within the Roun Groun world.
**Audience:** people who are dead serious about demanding adventure activities, extreme conditions and preparation.
**Category focus:** advanced electronics, safety-oriented equipment and specialised technical adventure gear. Electronics are central, not a minor accessory category.

**Character:** dead serious, precise, direct and purposeful. No jokes, playful copy, confetti, games, cute illustration or parent-brand leisure tone.

**Visual ambition:** ultra-futuristic industrial design; the frontier of modern equipment and pushing human limits. Dark presentation with electronics/circuitry imagery and intentional animation.

The website should behave as directly as it speaks. Visitors should understand the equipment’s purpose, status, limitations and next action without wading through hype.

## Owner-confirmed logo identity — 28 November 2026

**REDLINE always uses the fat/weighted Roun Groun wordmark. It must not use the original thin Roun Groun parent-brand logo.** This applies to the website, previews, headers, footers, watermarks, packaging and other REDLINE brand material. Keep the recognisable lettering, proportions and weight. A typed brand name or faux-bold treatment of the thin wordmark is not a substitute.

The owner supplied a bold reference earlier (`Banner 8.png`) and reiterated the weighted-only rule in the Radar Titanium revision request. Existing generated colour exports with rough/distressed edges are not an approved production master. RL-7 uses a clean vector silhouette traced from the coloured lettering of that owner-supplied reference for the concept; it does not declare those earlier generated exports approved. Use the owner's clean weighted masters when available, preserve the selected file and its provenance, and never silently substitute the thin mark.

The original thin mark embedded in earlier REDLINE explorations is superseded by this owner decision. This rule does not by itself authorise modifying live WordPress pages or unrelated assets.

## Proposed design language — starting point, not final approval

- Near-black graphite surfaces; controlled signal red; off-white primary text and legible grey supporting text.
- The sample concept palette uses graphite #08090B and signal red #FF342D. These are provisional until the owner approves a production design system.
- Large condensed display type, disciplined monospace metadata, strict alignment and restrained technical rules.
- Circuit traces, layered instrument displays, precise mechanical forms and real product photography where available.
- Motion should explain circuitry, device form or an interface state. Do not hide essential information behind cinematic sequences.
- Reduced-motion support, clear keyboard focus, readable contrast and responsive touch controls remain required.
- Original frontier-level design rather than copying a competitor’s identity.

A prior standalone homepage was a visual exploration, not a final approved site architecture, product catalogue or production specification.

## Proposed verbal direction

Short declarations. Concrete nouns and verbs. Clear product terminology. Technical details where useful, plain language where necessary.

Examples of concept copy, not approved trademarks or performance claims:

- Go beyond. Come back.
- Precision. Control. Return.
- Return is the objective.
- Complex inside. Decisive outside.

Avoid “fun”, “play”, cute banter and vague superiority claims. Seriousness must not become intimidation or glorification of reckless behaviour.

## Evidence and product truth

- Do not turn concept devices, speculative functions or rendered materials into claims about actual stock.
- VECTOR, BEACON and ATMOS in the sample were speculative design names, not confirmed shipping products.
- Never invent rescue connectivity, satellite coverage, positioning accuracy, survival guarantees, waterproof ratings, battery life, temperature ranges, field testing, certifications, endorsements or military adoption.
- Publish measured specifications and compatibility only with traceable supporting evidence.
- Identify simulations and prototypes clearly. A decorative “signal online” indicator must not imply real telemetry.
- Product limitations, intended use and relevant operating instructions must remain easy to find.
- A safety-oriented identity does not establish that a product is certified life-safety equipment.

## Brand boundaries

Do not copy the parent Roun Groun site, catalogue, support contacts, policies, checkout settings, analytics IDs or domains into REDLINE by assumption. Reuse must be deliberate, owner-authorized and recorded. No REDLINE-specific support address, company legal identity, manufacturing origin or policy terms are established by this brief.

## Decision record

Future changes must identify the owner instruction, date, responsible task, changed decision and affected assets/pages. Keep proposed and approved work distinguishable.
